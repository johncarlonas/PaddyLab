About the model.

This is the original CNN v2 from the student project. Three convolution blocks feed a dense classifier. Photos are resized to 256 × 256 pixels and normalized before prediction.

The model always chooses among these six labels, even for unrelated photos. Confidence is a softmax score. The notebook’s shuffled validation split needs an independent evaluation before reliable accuracy can be claimed.
